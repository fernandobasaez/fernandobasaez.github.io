AIjobSeeker

A personal, non-commercial job-search assistant.

AIjobSeeker is a private tool built and used by a single person to organise their own job search. It runs entirely on its owner's computer. It is not offered to the public, has no user accounts for other people and is not sold.

What it does

  • Collects job postings from public job sources and from the job-alert emails its owner receives.
  • Scores each posting against the owner's profile and helps tailor the owner's CV.
  • Tracks the owner's applications and notes replies from employers.

Use of Google data

With its owner's permission, the app reads the owner's own Gmail account in read-only fashion to find job-alert emails and replies from employers. It does not send, delete or change any email. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
