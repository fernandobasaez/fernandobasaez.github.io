Privacy Policy

AIjobSeeker · Last updated: 10 October 2026

AIjobSeeker is a personal application used by one person, its owner, to manage their own job search. It runs on the owner's own computer. This policy explains what data it accesses and how that data is handled.

Who uses the app

The app has a single user: its owner. It is not available to the public and does not collect data from anyone else.

Google user data the app accesses

With the owner's explicit consent through Google's sign-in screen, the app reads the owner's own Gmail account to:

  • Find job-alert emails (for example from LinkedIn) and extract the job title, company, location and link of each posting.
  • Find replies from employers to the owner's applications, using only the sender, subject line and the short preview that Gmail provides.

The app does not send, delete, label or modify any email, and does not read contacts, calendar or any other Google service.

How the data is stored

  • All data is stored only on the owner's computer, in a local database. Personal details are encrypted at rest.
  • Google sign-in credentials are stored only on the owner's computer and are never shared.
  • No data is sold, rented or used for advertising.

Sharing with third parties

Job posting details (title, company and description) may be sent to an AI model provider so the app can score postings and tailor the owner's CV. Personal contact details are removed before any such request. Email content from employers is not sent to any third party.

Limited use of Google data

AIjobSeeker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The owner can export or permanently delete all app data at any time from within the app, and can revoke the app's access to Gmail at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
